# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Potential employers, clients, and technical recruiters visiting the portfolio to evaluate Sam Cases' web development and design skills.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Sam Cases' work, experience, skills, and resume in a polished, responsive, and interactive way that encourages professional contact.

## Brand Personality
- Voice/Tone: Friendly, tech-forward, modern, and engaging.
- Style: Playful, minimalist, and highly polished.
- Emotional Goals: Instill trust in technical capabilities while delighting the visitor through high-quality micro-interactions.

## Anti-references
- AI Slop: Predictable, low-effort layouts, unpolished generic 3D effects, and illegible light gray text.
- Over-rounded elements (radii > 16px on containers).
- Unnecessarily busy layouts and cards repeated endlessly without hierarchy.

## Design Principles
1. Playful Tactility: Interactive features like the hero dice must look and feel physical, responsive, and polished.
2. Clean Hierarchy: Spacing and typography must be distinct, readable, and intentional.
3. Dark Mode First-Class: Seamless, robust transition between light and dark modes with color palettes stored cleanly in variables.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA standard compliance (sufficient contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for body copy).
- Proper layout adjustments and text scaling.
- prefers-reduced-motion support to respect user preferences for minimal motion.
